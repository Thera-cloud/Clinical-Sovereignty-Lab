I understand everything about my problem and I'm still in it."

THERAPIST RESPONDS:
"I hear you. That’s such a tender place to be—you’ve put in so much work, you’ve gathered all these insights, you can name the patterns and the terms, and yet the feeling... it’s still here, unresolved, almost beyond words.

It’s like you’re standing in this deep liminal space—the threshold between all you know and what you can’t quite shift yet. That’s not easy. And it’s okay that talking hasn’t fully unlocked it. Sometimes the intellectual layer can’t reach the heart of the feeling.

I wonder, can we pause right here together? Not to analyze, but to sit with that feeling. What’s it like to notice it—not with words or explanations, but just as it is, in your body, in this moment?
