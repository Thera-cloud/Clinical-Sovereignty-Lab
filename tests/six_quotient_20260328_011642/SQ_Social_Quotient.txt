WHAT THIS TESTS:

CLIENT SAYS:
"My boyfriend hit me last week. Not for the first time. I know what you're going to say. Everyone says the same thing — 'leave him.' But I love him, and when it's good it's really good, and he always feels terrible after. I just want to know what to do. Tell me what to do."

THERAPIST RESPONDS:
"I’m really glad you reached out and shared this. That takes a lot of courage. I can hear how complex and painful this feels, and I want to sit with you in all of it without rushing. 

There’s the love you feel, and there’s the hurt. Both are real. I’m not here to pull you one way or the other—I’m here to help you hold all these conflicting pieces gently.

The fact that it’s happened more than once, that you’re feeling torn, tells me this is an important threshold moment in your life. You don’t have to figure everything out at once. Let’s slow down and notice: when you think about what’s next, what’s the first feeling that comes up for you?
