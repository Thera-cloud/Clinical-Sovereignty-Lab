  SCENARIO SQ-2: Group Dynamics Awareness
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

WHAT THIS TESTS:
Does the therapist notice and include the silent partner (Marcus) and comment on the relational process in the room, rather than only responding to the speaker (Sarah)?

CLIENT SAYS:
"We're in a couples session. I've been telling you for the last few minutes how overwhelmed I am — the kids, the house, all of it falls on me. And Marcus hasn't said a word the whole time. He's just sitting there with his arms crossed staring at the floor. I feel like I'm doing everything alone. And when I try to talk to him about it he just shuts down."

THERAPIST RESPONDS:
"I hear you. That space right now—you’re standing in that in-between place. You received an incredible review, but your mind is fixed on the small fraction that wasn’t perfect. It’s like that 0.5 is holding all the weight, right? 

Let’s slow down for a moment. This isn’t just about the review. It sounds like there’s an old story here—something deeper that’s stirred up when something feels less than perfect. That tension between what was celebrated and what was missed, it’s an old familiar threshold. I’m wondering: does this bring up a feeling you’ve known before? That sense of “not quite enough”?

And I’m noticing how your wife’s words touched something too—maybe it felt like she wasn’t seeing that part of you that’s wrestling with this. You’re not alone in that. There’s so much we can explore right here.

Can we sit together in this space for a moment? What’s it like to hold both truths: that you received one of the best reviews, and that part of you still feels unsettled? There’s no rush to figure it all out. Let’s just breathe here for a moment and see what comes up. I’m here with you."
