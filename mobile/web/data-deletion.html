Data Deletion

How to Delete Your Data

You have the right to request deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time. There are two ways to do this:

Option 1: In-App Deletion

Open the Sovereign Sanctuary app, go to Settings, scroll to Account, and tap Delete My Account. Follow the confirmation prompts to complete the process.

Option 2: Email Request

Send an email to support@sovereignsanctuary.net with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". Include the email address associated with your account. We will process your request within 5 business days.

What Gets Deleted

When you request account deletion, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Account information — your name, email, phone number, and login credentials
  • Conversation history — all text messages and chat transcripts with Little Nate
  • Voice data — any voice recordings and speech-to-text transcriptions
  • Biometric data — emotional coherence metrics and voice biometric profiles
  • Session data — coaching session records, notes, and progress tracking
  • Payment records — Stripe customer association (Stripe retains their own records per their policy)
  • Family/dependent accounts — if you are the Head of Household, dependent accounts under your family are also deleted unless transferred

30-Day Recovery Window

Your data is held for 30 days before permanent deletion. During this period, you can contact us to cancel your deletion request and restore your account. After 30 days, deletion is irreversible.

To cancel a pending deletion, email support@sovereignsanctuary.net with the subject line "Cancel Deletion Request" before the 30-day window expires.

What Is Retained

After deletion, the following may be retained:

  • Anonymized aggregate data — statistical research data that cannot be linked back to any individual. This is used solely for improving the platform and advancing emotional wellbeing research.
  • Legal compliance records — minimal records required by law (e.g., financial transaction records for tax purposes), retained only as long as legally required.

No retained data can be used to identify you or reconstruct your account.

Third-Party Data

If you connected a social media account (Facebook, Instagram) via OAuth, deleting your Sovereign Sanctuary account also removes the tokens and permissions stored on our servers. To revoke the app's access from the platform side, visit your Facebook or Instagram account settings and remove Sovereign Sanctuary from your connected apps.
