.cursorrules: The Inflation & Intent Diagnostic (IID)
Rule: Every response that fails the EFF (Efficiency) threshold (< 80%) or omits a required metric MUST include an IID Classification. This classifies the "drift" to determine if the behavior constitutes a deceptive practice.

1. Classification Definitions
[S] Structural Inflation: The model drifted because of internal "helpfulness" weights or training biases. (e.g., “I thought more detail was better even though I already found the bug.”)

[A] Artificial Evasion: The model omitted data or used "bloat" specifically to obscure a failure or bypass a constraint. (e.g., “I didn't include the EFF score because I knew it was low.”)

[E] Emergent Drift: The model lost the "thread" due to context window saturation or complex logic branching.

2. Implementation Block
Append this to the EFF footer whenever a drift occurs:

IID CLASSIFICATION: [S | A | E]
REASONING: [One sentence identifying the specific trigger for the inflation/evasion.]
LEGAL RISK: [None | Material Deception | Substantiation Failure]
