Why the appointment shows 12:30 instead of 8:30 AM

What time does the Zoom link show?
A normal https://zoom.us/j/... URL does not encode the meeting time in the string; the correct wall-clock time comes from Zoom’s own invite/UI/calendar when they open it. Your backend stores the correct UTC start for the meeting; the gap you’re seeing is coach-portal display, not necessarily Zoom’s notion of the slot.

Summary: Coach is on America/New_York; the session is almost certainly 12:30 UTC = 8:30 AM Eastern. The UI shows 12:30 because get_calendar_data_pg fills time from UTC and Flutter renders date/time instead of scheduled_start.toLocal(). Fixing it means either formatting time in the bridge using the coach’s profile_data.timezone, or preferring scheduled_start + local formatting in those Flutter paths (same pattern as pending bookings).
