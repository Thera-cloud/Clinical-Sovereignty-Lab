Section C — Posting infrastructure
Adapters: post_content on facebook, instagram, linkedin, pinterest, reddit, x, tiktok, youtube (youtube effectively no real publish).
Session engine: skyeye_session_engine.py — _post_phase loads queue via SkyEyeContentGenerator.get_queue, then adapter.post_content(...); _create_phase also queues/pushes content. _get_platform_mode drives approval vs full autopilot (approval / full / etc.).
HTTP: skyeye_api.py — POST /api/skyeye/expressions/{id}/post (post_expression) calls get_adapter + authenticate + post_content with formatted text (minimal kwargs). Content queue: POST /api/skyeye/content-queue, …/approve, …/schedule — approve does not post by itself; posting happens when session _post_phase (or other workers) drain the queue.
Other: skyeye_chat.py can queue posts (queue_content); marketing_brain inserts queue rows for campaigns.
