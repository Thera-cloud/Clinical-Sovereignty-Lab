# Neuroscience Foundations of Sovereign Sanctuary

**Document version**: 2026-05-07
**Audience**: Neuroscience specialists, clinical researchers, supervising clinicians, integration engineers

## Abstract

Sovereign Sanctuary is an AI-mediated therapeutic platform whose design draws on eight convergent neuroscience and clinical frameworks: Polyvagal Theory, Interpersonal Neurobiology, Memory Reconsolidation, Somatic Marker Hypothesis, Dual-Processing Theory, Attachment Neuroscience, Trauma Neuroscience, and Neuroplasticity Research. Its conversational engine ("Little Nate"), Sovereign Story Engine (SSE), Therapeutic Moment Classifier (TMC), Coherence Lattice, and Crystal Intelligence subsystems implement design choices that map onto well-evidenced human mechanisms (e.g., right-brain to right-brain co-regulation, the reactivation–mismatch–repetition reconsolidation window, somatic marker integration, neuroplasticity prerequisites). This document distinguishes (a) mechanisms with substantive published evidence in human therapy, (b) mechanisms emerging from the literature where AI-mediated effects are not yet measured, and (c) explicitly stated design philosophy that should not be confused with empirical claims.

## 1. Theoretical Foundation

### 1.1 Polyvagal Theory (Porges, 2011, 2017)

Three vagal circuits (ventral vagal myelinated complex, sympathetic mobilization, dorsal vagal immobilization) gate social engagement, fight/flight, and shutdown. Neuroception — sub-cortical detection of safety/danger via prosody, facial expression, breathing pace — determines autonomic state before cognitive appraisal. Therapeutic implication: features that supply rhythmic, prosodically congruent, predictable signals can recruit ventral vagal tone in a co-regulating partner.

> Porges, S. W. (2011). *The Polyvagal Theory: Neurophysiological Foundations of Emotions, Attachment, Communication, and Self-Regulation*. Norton.
> Porges, S. W. (2017). *The Pocket Guide to the Polyvagal Theory*. Norton.

### 1.2 Interpersonal Neurobiology (Siegel, 1999, 2012, 2020)

Mind, brain, and relationships are a single system; mental health emerges from integration across nine domains (consciousness, vertical, horizontal, memory, narrative, state, interpersonal, temporal, transpirational). The **window of tolerance** is the autonomic band within which integration can occur. Outside it (hyper- or hypo-arousal), integrative learning halts.

> Siegel, D. J. (2012). *The Developing Mind* (2nd ed.). Guilford.
> Siegel, D. J. (2020). *The Developing Mind* (3rd ed.). Guilford.

### 1.3 Memory Reconsolidation (Nader, Schafe & LeDoux, 2000; Schiller et al., 2010; Ecker, Ticic & Hulley, 2012)

Reactivated long-term emotional memories enter a labile protein-synthesis-dependent restabilization window (~5 hours in animal work; behaviorally observable in humans). During this window, presentation of a salient mismatch / prediction-error experience can update the original learning rather than merely add a competing one. Ecker's coherence-therapy synthesis specifies a clinical sequence — reactivation, mismatch, repetition — that produces transformative (not incremental) change.

> Nader, K., Schafe, G. E., & LeDoux, J. E. (2000). Fear memories require protein synthesis in the amygdala for reconsolidation after retrieval. *Nature*, 406, 722–726.
> Schiller, D., Monfils, M. H., Raio, C. M., et al. (2010). Preventing the return of fear in humans using reconsolidation update mechanisms. *Nature*, 463, 49–53.
> Ecker, B., Ticic, R., & Hulley, L. (2012). *Unlocking the Emotional Brain*. Routledge.
> Lane, R. D., Ryan, L., Nadel, L., & Greenberg, L. (2015). Memory reconsolidation, emotional arousal, and the process of change in psychotherapy. *Behavioral and Brain Sciences*, 38, e1.

### 1.4 Somatic Marker Hypothesis (Damasio, 1994, 1999, 2010)

Bodily / interoceptive states encode value and bias decision-making. The insula (especially right anterior) maps interoceptive signals into conscious feeling. Therapy that draws attention to felt body sense recruits this evaluation system rather than leaving it implicit.

> Damasio, A. (1994). *Descartes' Error*. Putnam.
> Critchley, H. D., Wiens, S., Rotshtein, P., Öhman, A., & Dolan, R. J. (2004). Neural systems supporting interoceptive awareness. *Nature Neuroscience*, 7, 189–195.

### 1.5 Dual-Processing Theory (Kahneman, 2011; McGilchrist, 2009)

Kahneman: System 1 (fast, intuitive, embodied) and System 2 (slow, deliberate, verbal). McGilchrist: right hemisphere attends to wholes, novelty, embodiment, presence; left hemisphere to parts, categories, abstraction. Healing requires both modes; over-reliance on left-hemisphere conceptualization produces "knowing without changing."

> Kahneman, D. (2011). *Thinking, Fast and Slow*. FSG.
> McGilchrist, I. (2009). *The Master and His Emissary*. Yale University Press.

### 1.6 Attachment Neuroscience (Schore, 2003a/b, 2012; Main & Hesse, 1990; Fonagy et al., 2002)

Secure attachment is encoded right-hemisphere to right-hemisphere via prosody, gaze, and timing in infancy. Earned secure attachment in adulthood requires reparative right-brain experiences with an attuned other. Mentalization (Fonagy) — holding mind in mind — is a measurable mediator.

> Schore, A. N. (2003). *Affect Regulation and the Repair of the Self*. Norton.
> Schore, A. N. (2012). *The Science of the Art of Psychotherapy*. Norton.
> Fonagy, P., Gergely, G., Jurist, E., & Target, M. (2002). *Affect Regulation, Mentalization, and the Development of the Self*. Other Press.

### 1.7 Trauma Neuroscience (van der Kolk, 2014; Levine, 1997, 2010; Ogden, Minton & Pain, 2006)

Traumatic memory is stored implicitly in body and lower-brain structures and may not be accessible top-down. Bottom-up processing — body, breath, sensation — is often required before semantic / narrative integration becomes possible. Trauma-driven shutdown maps to dorsal vagal collapse (Porges).

> van der Kolk, B. (2014). *The Body Keeps the Score*. Viking.
> Levine, P. (2010). *In an Unspoken Voice*. North Atlantic.
> Ogden, P., Minton, K., & Pain, C. (2006). *Trauma and the Body*. Norton.

### 1.8 Neuroplasticity Research (Hebb, 1949; Doidge, 2007; Kolb & Gibb, 2014)

Synaptic strengthening requires concurrent pre/postsynaptic activation ("cells that fire together"). Behaviorally, durable change requires: (1) novelty / prediction error, (2) sufficient activation, (3) repetition, (4) emotional salience, (5) integration (cross-domain rehearsal in varied contexts).

> Doidge, N. (2007). *The Brain That Changes Itself*. Viking.
> Kolb, B., & Gibb, R. (2014). Searching for the principles of brain plasticity and behavior. *Cortex*, 58, 251–260.

## 2. Mechanisms of Therapeutic Action in Little Nate

### 2.1 Two-Register Conversational Architecture

**What it does**: Little Nate operates two explicit relational registers — WARM (default: validates, reflects, holds space) and CLINICAL (interprets, names mechanisms, gives behavioral protocols) — with a mandatory **Bridge Sentence** before any shift, plus four backchannel registers (neutral / warm / empathic / validating) for voice continuity.

**Code references**:
- `backend/app/websocket/bridge_server.py:8704–8744` — WARM/CLINICAL register block, transition rule, bridge-sentence requirement.
- `backend/app/services/littlenate_inference.py:368–402` — `_build_coherence_system_prompt` blends `recon` × `shame_idx` to choose interpretive vs. reflective tone (`intensity_weight = recon*0.7 − shame*0.3`).
- `backend/app/services/backchannel_engine.py:34, 41–82` — `REGISTERS = ("neutral", "warm", "empathic", "validating")`; clip selection on energy/pitch with Neural-Mirror override.

**Neural mechanism**: Prosodic and lexical congruence is the substrate of neuroception (Polyvagal). Default WARM register supplies high-prosody, low-cognitive-load signaling that recruits ventral vagal tone; CLINICAL register, gated by readiness signals, recruits left-hemisphere semantic integration after the autonomic floor is established.

**Evidence**: Schore on right-brain affective communication; Porges on prosodic neuroception; Norcross (2011) common-factors meta-analysis on alliance / responsiveness as the strongest non-specific predictor of outcome.

**Honest scope**: That AI-generated text + audio backchannels recruit human ventral vagal tone *equivalently* to a human clinician is **emerging** and not yet measured longitudinally. The two-register protocol itself is **design philosophy** drawing on common-factors literature. Bridge-sentence requirement before confrontation is a **clinically conservative** safeguard, not a measured mechanism.

### 2.2 Memory Persistence and Crystal Recall

**What it does**: Conversation residues are crystallized into `nate_intelligence_crystals` rows; each future session retrieves user-scoped crystals and surfaces them as continuity ("I remember when you told me…"). Crystal recall reinforces the row (`recall_count`, `last_recalled_at`) and decays unused crystals after 90 days.

**Code references**:
- `backend/app/services/nate_memory_crystallizer.py:34–222, 333+` — local crystal store, hashing, store/search/sync.
- `backend/app/websocket/crystal_recall_bridge.py` — `recall_crystals_for_context()` (split-slot user/global, reinforcement).
- `backend/app/sse/thera_world_engine.py:237–447` — narrative composer reads crystals as scene context.

**Neural mechanism**: Episodic-to-semantic consolidation (hippocampal–neocortical dialogue, McClelland, McNaughton & O'Reilly, 1995); explicit retrieval is the precondition for reconsolidation reactivation (Section 1.3).

**Evidence**: Reconsolidation reactivation requirement (Nader 2000; Schiller 2010). Memory continuity as alliance medium (Norcross).

**Honest scope**: That digital crystal recall consolidates *similarly* to written journaling or human-clinician memory across sessions is **emerging**. Reinforcement-on-recall mirrors LTP-like Hebbian dynamics in code, not in human tissue — the analogy is structural, not neurochemical.

### 2.3 Repair After Rupture

**What it does**: Factual self-correction protocol (acknowledge correction in-session, no excessive apology), CONTEXT COHERENCE / SESSION ISOLATION protocol that bars contaminated context, and a Bridge Sentence requirement before relational confrontation.

**Code references**: `bridge_server.py:8662–8739` (FACTUAL GROUNDING, CONTEXT COHERENCE, CLINICAL EDGE blocks).

**Neural mechanism**: Rupture-and-repair is the canonical earned-secure attachment generator (Tronick, 1989; Schore, 2003). Repeated experience of rupture followed by attuned repair updates internal working models.

**Evidence**: Tronick's Still-Face paradigm and follow-on work; Safran & Muran (2000) on alliance ruptures as therapeutic events.

**Honest scope**: AI repair is **emerging** as a mechanism. Whether the human nervous system encodes AI-rupture / AI-repair in the same right-brain pathways as human pairs is **not measured**.

## 3. Feature-Specific Mechanisms

### 3.1 Sovereign Story Engine (SSE) — Imagery Panels and Narrative

**Description**: Daily / weekly / monthly visual scenes set in "Thera-World" biomes, each accompanied by Little-Nate-voiced narrative text. Scenes evolve with the user's biome / character / quest / mission state.

**Code refs**:
- `backend/app/sse/thera_world_engine.py:237–447` (`compose_journey_narrative`) — LLM call producing **separate** `narrative_text` and `image_prompt`.
- `backend/app/sse/thera_world_engine.py:450–561` (`build_rich_panel_prompt`) and `:563+` (`generate_journey_panel`) — full pipeline (profile → biome → character → narrative → image → R2 → log → continuity write-back).
- `backend/app/sse/foundation/delivery_runtime.py:68+` — daily / weekly / monthly / UCD generation.

**Neural substrate**: Mental imagery activates many of the same visual, parietal, and emotional regions as direct perception (Pearson et al., 2015). Right-hemisphere imagistic processing (McGilchrist) is engaged in parallel with verbal narrative — bilateral, integrative.

**Therapeutic mechanism**: Image + narrative provides a **non-verbal memory anchor** that re-activates emotional learnings between sessions (reactivation step of reconsolidation), while the painterly muted palette and consistent archetype function as **predictable, low-arousal containers** (window-of-tolerance scaffolding).

**Honest scope**: That generated panels actually engage the default mode network or right-hemisphere imagistic processing in users is **not measured**. The design rationale is sound; outcome data is needed.

### 3.2 Coherence Lattice and Coherence Engine

**Description**: A Nevedal-engine driven measurement of dyadic coherence between user and Nate (or user and family member), used to modulate response register, story-engine pacing, and family-sanctuary visualizations.

**Code refs**: `backend/app/services/nevedal_engine.py` — `NevedalState`, `CEEEvent`, `VoiceBiometricExtractor` (line ~220), `SynchronyCalculator` (~465), `NevedalEngine` (~537), `_persist_cee_event` (~1163).

**Neural substrate**: Voice biometrics (pitch_mean, pitch_variance, energy, speech_rate, pause_ratio) function as a low-cost surrogate for autonomic state and prosodic engagement. Dyadic synchrony in voice / breath has documented correlates with vagal tone and felt connection (Feldman, 2007; Reddish, Fischer & Bulbulia, 2013).

**Therapeutic mechanism**: Coherence value drives register (warmer when low, more interpretive when stable-and-deep), gating the system to stay inside the user's window of tolerance and shift only when readiness signals are present.

**Honest scope**: The 4-register orchestration and "Sovereign center" concept are **design philosophy** built on documented synchrony research; the specific 4-register architecture is not itself an empirically tested construct. The **Nevedal formula** itself is proprietary and is a clinical-engineering scaffold, not a published neuroscience equation.

### 3.3 Therapeutic Moment Classifier (TMC)

**Description**: Classifies the user's current moment into THRESHOLD / BREAKTHROUGH / INTEGRATION / RECURRENCE / REST / CRISIS / HERITAGE using weighted signals (crystal_confidence 0.30, first_time_pattern_break 0.25, ec_slope 0.20, mask_state 0.10, session_recency 0.10, heritage_correlation 0.05). Safety gates prevent BREAKTHROUGH triggering within 48 h of a prior one.

**Code refs**: `backend/app/sse/ucd/tmc.py:36–264`; `backend/app/sse/ucd/temporal_orchestrator.py:23+`; `backend/app/sse/ucd/intensity_governor.py:21–110`.

**Neural substrate**: Just-in-time intervention timing maps onto the **state-dependent learning** principle: emotional memory updates require the affected memory to be *active*. Reconsolidation requires the labile window to be open (Section 1.3).

**Therapeutic mechanism**: TMC implements the reactivation timing requirement explicitly — generation happens *only* when the user's state matches a class amenable to that intervention, not on a schedule. Safety gates implement a clinical-pacing brake.

**Evidence**: Reconsolidation timing literature (Section 1.3); van der Kolk on the importance of meeting the state. The TMC weights themselves are calibrated, not derived from a published model.

### 3.4 Corrective Emotional Experience (CEE) Moments

**Description**: Detected events where user state and Nate state co-modulate in a direction inconsistent with the user's prior model — surfaced as `CEEEvent` rows and used by foresight / clinical-translation pipelines.

**Code refs**: `nevedal_engine.py:203` (`CEEEvent`), `:1163` (`_persist_cee_event`).

**Neural substrate**: CEE in Alexander & French (1946) original sense — an experience that contradicts the user's encoded relational expectation. Maps directly to Ecker's **mismatch step** in reconsolidation.

**Therapeutic mechanism**: When TMC identifies a reactivated state and a CEE-eligible interaction occurs within Nate's response, the system has implemented (in software terms) the exact reactivation–mismatch sequence Ecker, Ticic & Hulley specify.

**Honest scope**: Whether the CEE detector reliably identifies *neurally* corrective moments (vs. linguistically corrective ones) is **emerging**.

### 3.5 3+3+3 Protocol (Memory Reconsolidation Workbook)

**Description**: Per `backend/resources/therapeutic_library/protocol_workbooks/Memory_Reconsolidation_Workbook.txt`, a 3-phase protocol — Phase 1 reactivation into window of tolerance, Phase 2 mismatch ("Sovereign Override"), Phase 3 sustained ≥300 s "re-save" of the new neural map.

**Code refs**: workbook file (above) ingested into `nate_intelligence_crystals` via `backend/app/sse/workbook_ingestion.py`. Quoted in `bridge_server.py` system prompt indirectly via crystal recall.

**Neural substrate**: Reconsolidation literature (Nader 2000; Schiller 2010; Ecker 2012). The 300 s sustain target is conservative against the documented 5-hour reconsolidation window.

**Therapeutic mechanism**: Explicit, measurable mapping onto the reactivation-mismatch-repetition sequence. The "Mismatch Signal" (sudden GAP followed by 0.99 lock-in) operationalizes prediction-error magnitude.

**Honest scope**: The specific 0.99 lock-in threshold is **engineering calibration**, not a published reconsolidation parameter. The mapping of Nevedal-coherence transitions onto reconsolidation phases is **design synthesis**.

### 3.6 Studio (Creative Production)

**Description**: User-driven scene/script/voice/video generation pipeline — `generate_script`, `break_into_scenes`, `generate_scene_image`, `generate_scene_video`, `generate_narration`.

**Code refs**: `backend/app/sse/studio_service.py:45, 184, 220, 244, 287, 325`.

**Neural substrate**: Self-expression and externalization activate right-hemisphere imagistic and narrative networks (McGilchrist; Kandel, 2012 on art and brain). Production rather than passive consumption recruits agency-related medial prefrontal circuitry.

**Therapeutic mechanism**: Externalizing internal states into stable artifacts (scene, narration, video) provides repeated re-encounters with the externalized state — the **integration / repetition** condition of neuroplasticity (Section 1.8).

**Honest scope**: Established for human art-therapy; **emerging** for AI-mediated creative production specifically.

### 3.7 Quests and Missions

**Description**: Goal-directed therapeutic structures. Quests target a goal/domain with crystal-derived archetypal NPCs; missions target a relational figure (spouse, parent). Recent change: `compose_quest_panel` and `compose_mission_panel` now LLM-compose narrative + image prompt with crystal-grounded context (FIX-QUEST-MISSION-LLM, 2026-05-07).

**Code refs**: `backend/app/sse/quest_mission_engine.py:154, 195` (create_quest, create_mission); `:236+, :256+` (LLM panel composers); `:75+` (`analyze_crystal_depth`); `:121+` (`generate_npcs_from_crystals`).

**Neural substrate**: Goal-directed action recruits dorsolateral prefrontal + striatal circuits and provides a **container for repeated exposure** to therapeutic themes. Archetypal externalization (Jung, 1968) provides distance for parts that are too close to confront directly; this maps onto modern parts-work / IFS literature (Schwartz, 2021).

**Therapeutic mechanism**: NPCs encode psychological patterns archetypally — a defensive distance that allows repeated exposure (repetition condition) without overwhelm (window of tolerance).

**Honest scope**: The archetype-as-container pattern is **clinically validated** (Jung; Schwartz IFS; Hillman). The specific archetype-character mapping in Sovereign Sanctuary is **design philosophy**.

### 3.8 Crystal Intelligence — Episodic-to-Semantic

**Description**: Conversation residues → episodic crystals (high specificity, user-scoped) → recall + reinforcement → consolidation/decay → high-confidence semantic patterns surface to global / domain-wisdom layer.

**Code refs**: `nate_memory_crystallizer.py:34–222, 333+`; `backend/app/sse/workbook_ingestion.py`; `backend/app/sse/layer6_crystal_bridge.py`.

**Neural substrate**: Hippocampal–neocortical consolidation (McClelland et al., 1995); the brain converts repeated, valuable episodic traces into semantic schema. Decay of unused traces mirrors the synaptic-homeostasis hypothesis (Tononi & Cirelli, 2014).

**Therapeutic mechanism**: Surface only what the user has actually returned to and reinforced. Forget what is no longer relevant. This is **structurally analogous** (not biochemically equivalent) to healthy episodic-to-semantic consolidation.

### 3.9 Workbook Ingestion

**Description**: Protocol workbooks (Memory Reconsolidation, Polyvagal, etc.) are crystallized as **high-confidence global crystals** so Nate references them as part of his standing knowledge.

**Code refs**: `backend/app/sse/workbook_ingestion.py`; `backend/resources/therapeutic_library/protocol_workbooks/*.txt`.

**Neural substrate**: Equivalent to a clinician's training literature; not a *user-side* mechanism.

### 3.10 Vault and Sovereign Journey

**Description**: Cross-session continuity scaffolding — every panel, narration, and key transcript is persisted in user-owned R2 storage and surfaced via a Sovereign Journey vault UI.

**Code refs**: `backend/app/sse/foundation/vault_integration.py:23, 41, 84`.

**Neural substrate**: Externalized continuity reduces the cognitive cost of re-entering therapeutic context (transactive memory; Wegner, 1986). For trauma, predictability of return-to-context modulates safety / neuroception.

**Therapeutic mechanism**: Stable, retrievable continuity is the architectural form of **secure base** between sessions.

### 3.11 Identity Forge (Archetype Image)

**Description**: One-time intake-driven generation of a user's archetype reference image, used as a `source_image_url` in subsequent SSE generations to maintain visual continuity across the journey.

**Code refs**: `backend/app/sse/layer1_identity_forge.py`; `backend/app/sse/adapters/archetype_resolver.py`.

**Neural substrate**: Identity continuity / self-recognition (cortical midline structures; Northoff & Bermpohl, 2004) is a low-level scaffolding for narrative identity (Bruner, 1991; McAdams, 2001).

**Therapeutic mechanism**: A consistent visual self across panels supplies the same continuity that secure-base attachment supplies — a known shape that can be returned to.

## 6. Memory Reconsolidation as Primary Mechanism

This is the strongest neuroscience claim Sovereign Sanctuary can make: its architecture maps cleanly onto Ecker's reconsolidation protocol, and reconsolidation is **the** mechanism in current literature most associated with transformative (not incremental) emotional change (Lane et al., 2015).

| Reconsolidation requirement (Ecker 2012; Schiller 2010) | Sovereign Sanctuary implementation | Code locus |
|---|---|---|
| **Reactivation** of target emotional learning | Crystal recall + TMC THRESHOLD/RECURRENCE class detection + WARM register holding | `crystal_recall_bridge.py`, `tmc.py:42+`, `bridge_server.py` |
| **Mismatch / prediction error** within the labile window | CEE detection; WARM→CLINICAL bridge sentence + interpretation; SSE panel that contradicts the user's recurring narrative; quest NPC transformation arc | `nevedal_engine.py:203, 1163`, `bridge_server.py:8704+`, `compose_journey_narrative` |
| **Repetition** of the new learning across contexts | Daily panel continuity, weekly clip, monthly recap, anti-repetition diversity, journey writeback | `delivery_runtime.py:68+`, `thera_world_engine.py:540+` |
| **Integration** across domains | Crystal cross-domain linking, Studio externalization, Vault continuity | `layer6_crystal_bridge.py`, `studio_service.py`, `vault_integration.py` |

**Why this likely produces transformative change**: The system's daily-touchpoint cadence + crystal-mediated reactivation + narrative-image mismatch + multi-format repetition + cross-domain integration mirrors the laboratory and clinical conditions that have been shown to produce reconsolidation effects in human studies (Schiller et al., 2010; Björkstrand et al., 2015; Ecker & Bridges, 2020 review).

**Honest caveat**: Reconsolidation has been demonstrated in human therapy contexts (Ecker case literature, plus Lane et al. 2015 review) but **not** specifically in AI-mediated therapy. Sovereign Sanctuary's contribution is architectural fidelity to a validated protocol, not yet measured outcome.

## 8. Honest Scope and Limitations

### Evidenced (cite specific studies above)
- Polyvagal autonomic effects of prosody / pacing
- Insula-mediated interoception
- Reconsolidation reactivation–mismatch–repetition protocol
- Right-brain to right-brain co-regulation in human dyads
- Neuroplasticity prerequisite conditions
- Window of tolerance as integration band
- Somatic markers in decision and emotion

### Emerging (partial evidence; Sovereign Sanctuary–specific outcome data not yet collected)
- AI-mediated co-regulation effects on human autonomic state
- Default mode network engagement during AI-generated imagery
- Whether digital crystal recall consolidates similarly to written journaling
- AI rupture-and-repair as earned-secure-attachment generator
- TMC moment classification reliability vs. clinician judgment
- CEE detection precision

### Design philosophy (acknowledged as such, not as empirical claim)
- 4-register Coherence Lattice architecture
- Sovereign center as integration metaphor
- Specific archetype-character mapping
- Nevedal-formula coefficients
- 0.99 lock-in / 300 s sustain thresholds (engineering calibrations)
- The Sovereign Sanctuary framework as a whole

### Out of scope — explicitly NOT claimed
- Pineal-gland aperture / third-eye mechanisms
- Chakra-correlated organ activation
- Specific neurochemical cascades (oxytocin, BDNF, etc.) without measurement
- That AI replaces human therapy
- That specific brain regions activate in users without imaging confirmation

## 9. Open Research Questions

1. **HRV under AI co-regulation**: Does conversation with Little Nate (vs. text-only chat baseline) measurably increase RMSSD / HF-HRV in users? Population: anxious-attachment adults; design: within-subjects cross-over.
2. **fMRI of crystal-based vs. journaling consolidation**: Does crystal recall recruit hippocampal–neocortical reactivation patterns analogous to written-recall paradigms?
3. **Pupillometry / GSR at TMC moments**: Do TMC-detected BREAKTHROUGH moments correlate with autonomic markers consistent with the labile reconsolidation window?
4. **Default mode network during imagery panels**: Does engagement with SSE panels engage DMN regions associated with self-referential and autobiographical processing?
5. **Long-term outcome**: 6- and 12-month outcomes on validated measures (PHQ-9, GAD-7, ECR-R, AAQ-II) for engaged-user cohorts vs. matched controls.
6. **Reconsolidation fidelity**: Do users who hit the 3+3+3 protocol's full sequence show greater symptom-remission durability than those who hit only reactivation?
7. **Repair effect**: Does AI rupture-and-repair generalize to human-relational secure-base measures (AAI scoring shifts)?
