# Strategic Narrative
### EAP Failure, Presenteeism Costs, and the Talent War

---

**The EAP Utilization Gap**

Traditional Employee Assistance Programs achieve 3–5% utilization. You contract for phone trees, referrals, and maybe a handful of counseling sessions—and 95% of your workforce never touches it. Meanwhile, depression costs $3,400 per employee per year in lost productivity (WHO). Burnout affects 76% of workers. Presenteeism—showing up but not fully engaged—drains more value than absenteeism. Your wellness spend is invisible. Your risk is not.

---

**The Presenteeism Paradox**

Employees who are struggling don't call EAPs. They show up. They log in. They deliver 60% of their capacity. They leave eventually—or worse, they stay and spread low morale. Traditional EAPs are built for the moment of crisis. They're not built for the 364 days of low-grade stress, anxiety, and emotional depletion that lead there.

---

**The Talent War**

In a tight labor market, benefits that employees *actually use* become retention tools. "We have an EAP" doesn't differentiate. "We give you an AI companion who knows your name, learns your patterns, and is there at 2 a.m." does. Sovereign Sanctuary Corporate reframes wellness from a checkbox to a competitive advantage.

> **Key Takeaway:** EAPs fail because they're designed for the crisis moment, not the daily reality. Sovereign Sanctuary meets employees where they are—24/7, stigma-free, measurable.
