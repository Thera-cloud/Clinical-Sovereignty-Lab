## NARRATIVE: The Crisis You Already Know

**The readmission crisis.** 20% of Medicare patients are readmitted within 30 days. CMS penalties have turned preventable readmissions into a direct hit to margin. Hospitalists and medical directors are held accountable for outcomes they can't see after discharge.

**The discharge black hole.** Patients leave with a folder, a prescription, and a phone number. No emotional support. No automated check-in. No early warning. By the time they present in the ED, the window for preventive intervention has closed.

**The medication non-adherence epidemic.** NEHI estimates $100–290B/year in avoidable costs from non-adherence. It drives readmissions, morbidity, and mortality—yet systematic follow-up is impossible at scale with current staffing.

**Caregiver burden.** Families are overwhelmed. They don't know when to escalate. They lack coordination. Discharge planning assumes a support system that often doesn't exist.

**HCAHPS pressure.** Satisfaction scores are tied to reimbursement. The discharge experience—clarity, follow-up, responsiveness—directly impacts your bottom line. One bad experience cascades.

**The nursing shortage.** Even when you want to call every post-discharge patient, you can't. Nurses are stretched. Follow-up calls are deprioritized. The gap widens.

---

> **Key Takeaway:** The problem isn't intent—it's structural. You need a continuity layer that operates at scale, 24/7, without adding headcount.

## ECOSYSTEM: How It Works

**Medical dashboard.** A care-team coordination portal where hospitalists, nurses, PAs, and social workers view patient engagement, risk flags, and voice-derived distress indicators. Role-based access; HIPAA-aware architecture.

**Patient app.** Inpatient and post-discharge use. Little Nate—the AI companion—guides patients through check-ins, medication reminders, symptom questions, and emotional support. Available on mobile and web.

**Post-discharge AI companion.** Automated check-ins at configurable intervals. Conversation-driven rather than form-driven. Surfaces barriers to adherence, distress, and confusion.

**Voice biometrics for pain/distress.** Patent-pending Nevedal Formula C_emo(t) extracts pitch, energy, speech rate, pause ratio, and stress index from voice. Early warning signals for decompensation—before the patient calls the ED.

**Medication adherence check-ins.** Automated prompts that engage patients conversationally. Flags non-adherence patterns for care-team follow-up.

**Family Sanctuary.** Caregiver coordination hub. Shared updates, progress visibility, and escalation paths—without overloading clinical staff.

**Readmission risk scoring.** CEE (Coherent Emotional Engagement) trends feed risk models. High-risk patients surface for proactive outreach.

**Care team coordination portal.** Master Coach (Hospitalist/Medical Director) and Assistant Coaches (nurses, PAs, residents, social workers) collaborate on patient cohorts with unified visibility.
