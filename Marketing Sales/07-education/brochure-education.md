# Sovereign Sanctuary Education
## Your Campus Has a 6-Week Waitlist. Your Students Are Struggling Tonight.

---

### The Problem

**60%** of college students report anxiety or depression (ACHA). Campus counseling centers average **6-week waitlists**. The ratio hovers at **1 counselor per 1,500+ students**—while IACS recommends 1:250. Title IX and ADA compliance gaps leave institutions exposed when mental health crises go unaddressed. After hours, on weekends, during finals: students are alone. The system is built for scarcity; your students need support that scales.

---

### The Solution

**Sovereign Sanctuary Education** is an AI-powered therapy platform designed for higher education. Little Nate—a 24/7 AI companion—is available to every student, while Deans and Student Services Directors (Master Coaches) and campus counselors/RAs (Assistant Coaches) gain visibility, training, and crisis detection tools. No waitlists. No after-hours gaps. No compliance guessing.
