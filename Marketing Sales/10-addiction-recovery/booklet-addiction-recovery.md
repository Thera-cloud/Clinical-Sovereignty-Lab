# THE RELAPSE CRISIS

The addiction treatment industry has accepted a brutal statistic: **40–60% of individuals in recovery relapse within the first year** (NIDA). Readmission rates climb higher when follow-up care is fragmented. Families and treatment centers alike brace for the "revolving door"—discharge, return, discharge, return.

Three structural failures drive this cycle:

1. **Between-session vulnerability** — Relapse rarely happens in the clinician's office. It happens alone. At night. On weekends. Between scheduled appointments when support is unavailable.

2. **Self-report unreliability** — "How are you doing?" depends on willingness and ability to answer honestly. Shame, denial, and memory gaps distort the picture. By the time a client admits struggle, crisis may already be underway.

3. **Alumni dropout** — Treatment centers invest heavily in residential and outpatient programs, then lose contact with graduates. Formal follow-up attrition exceeds 80% within 90 days. No visibility means no intervention.

The cost is measured in dollars ($10K–$30K per readmission), clinician burnout, and—most importantly—lives and families disrupted by preventable relapse.

> **Key Takeaway:** Relapse isn't random. It's predictable—if you have the right signals and continuous visibility.

**Challenge:** High readmission rates. Alumni disappear after discharge. No visibility into post-discharge risk.

**Solution:** Onboard clients during treatment. Configure Clinical Director as Master Coach, recovery staff as Assistant Coaches. # OPERATIONS

## Implementation Path

| Phase | Activities |
|------|------------|
| **Clinical Staff Training** | 2–4 hour orientation. Dashboard navigation, alert interpretation, voice biometric literacy. Integration with existing clinical workflows. |
| **Client Onboarding (During Treatment)** | Clients onboarded before discharge. AI companion and DOJO introduced in treatment curriculum. Consent for voice analysis and sponsor visibility documented. |
| **Sponsor / Assistant Coach Setup** | Recovery coaches and sponsors invited as Assistant Coaches. Role-based access. Clear boundaries and HIPAA-aligned data sharing. |
| **Transition to Post-Discharge** | Handoff protocol from treatment team to post-discharge monitoring. First 30 days highest-touch. Automated check-ins and milestone reminders. |
| **Ongoing Monitoring** | Continuous voice biometric assessment. Alert escalation. Regular outcome reviews and trend analysis. |

## Governance

- **Master Coach** = Clinical Director. Full visibility. Configurable alerts.
- **Assistant Coach** = Recovery coaches, sponsors. Client-specific access. Alerts per client assignment.
- **Client** = Individual in recovery. AI companion, DOJO, peer mesh, meeting logs, milestone tracking.

> **Key Takeaway:** Implementation is phased and role-based. Clinical governance remains with licensed staff; AI and biometrics augment—never replace—human judgment.
