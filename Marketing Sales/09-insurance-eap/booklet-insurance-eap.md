# The EAP Utilization Crisis

### What the Numbers Say

- **3–5% utilization** (EASNA) — Most covered lives never engage.
- **$1,500–3,000 per employee per year** — Mental health benefits paid, largely unused.
- **Zero outcome visibility** — Payers don't know if interventions work.
- **Unverifiable network quality** — Therapist credentials exist; outcomes don't.

### Employee Dissatisfaction

Employees report confusion, long wait times, and a "call this number" experience that feels transactional and anonymous. Stigma and access barriers keep them from engaging—until crisis.

### Employer Frustration

Employers pay premiums, see low utilization, and have nothing to show boards or HR committees. Benefits teams can't answer: *Are we getting value? Are our people getting better?*

**The market is ready for a different approach.**
