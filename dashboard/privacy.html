PART II — PRIVACY POLICY

12. DATA WE COLLECT

Sovereign Sanctuary collects and processes the following categories of data:

a) Account Information

  • Name, email address, phone number, date of birth
  • Username and encrypted password
  • Emergency contact information
  • Timezone preference
  • Profile photograph (if provided)

b) Voice Biometric Data

  • Pitch mean and variance
  • Vocal energy levels
  • Speech rate (words per minute)
  • Pause ratio and silence patterns
  • Voiceprint characteristics for emotional state analysis

c) Facial Geometry Data (Sovereign Circle tier only)

  • Facial expression analysis via video during Avatar Mode sessions
  • Micro-expression detection for emotional coherence measurement
  • This data is processed in real-time and is NOT stored as raw video

d) Text and Conversation Data

  • All messages exchanged with Little Nate
  • Session transcripts from coaching sessions
  • Family Sanctuary group session content
  • Homework assignments and journal entries

e) Emotional and Analytical Data

  • Emotional coherence scores (C_emo values)
  • CEE Window detection events
  • Crisis risk assessments
  • Predictability Model of Behavior profiles
  • Mood tracking and longitudinal emotional trajectories
  • Night School training data and AI improvement metrics

f) Technical and Usage Data

  • Device identifiers (hardware_id)
  • IP addresses (for consent verification)
  • Session timestamps and duration
  • Token usage and billing records
  • Login history and feature usage patterns

13. HOW WE PROCESS YOUR DATA

Your data is processed using the following third-party services:

  • Azure OpenAI (Microsoft): Text analysis, voice processing, and AI conversation generation. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and processed under Microsoft's enterprise data protection agreements. Your data is NOT used to train OpenAI's general models.
  • Stripe: Payment processing for subscriptions and coaching fees. Sovereign Sanctuary does not store credit card numbers directly.
  • PostgreSQL Database: Account data, session metadata, and analytical scores are stored in encrypted databases hosted on secured infrastructure.
  • Redis: Temporary session caching for real-time features.

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Voice biometric data is processed in real-time and stored only as extracted feature vectors, not as raw audio recordings (unless you explicitly opt in to session recording for coaching review purposes).

14. DATA RETENTION

  • Active accounts: Data is retained for the duration of your membership
  • Deleted accounts: Upon account deletion request, your data is held for thirty (30) days in a recoverable state. If you do not reactivate within 30 days, all personally identifiable data is permanently purged
  • Anonymized aggregate data: De-identified, aggregate analytical data may be retained indefinitely for research purposes. This data cannot be traced back to any individual user
  • Legal holds: Data subject to legal proceedings or regulatory requirements may be retained beyond the standard retention period as required by law
  • Coaching records: Coaches' session notes may be retained separately under their professional record-keeping obligations

15. DATA SHARING

Your data is NEVER sold. We share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Your assigned Coach: Session summaries, emotional state trends, crisis alerts, and pre-session briefs are shared with your assigned coach to support your coaching relationship
  • Head of Household: In family accounts, the Head of Household may view aggregate family wellness metrics. Individual session content is NOT shared with other family members
  • Law enforcement: Only when legally compelled by valid court order, subpoena, or other mandatory legal process
  • Research publication: Anonymized, aggregate data may be used in published research. No individual user is ever identifiable in published research

16. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

a) California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)
You have the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion of your data, opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell data), and not be discriminated against for exercising your rights. To exercise these rights, use the "Delete My Account" feature in Settings or contact support.

b) Illinois Residents (BIPA)
We collect voice biometric data as described in Section 12(b). By accepting this agreement, you provide written consent to the collection, use, and storage of your voiceprint data for the purpose of emotional state analysis. This data is retained only for the duration of your active account plus the 30-day deletion grace period.

c) Texas Residents (CUBI)
You are notified that we collect biometric identifiers including voiceprint and (for eligible tiers) facial geometry data. This data is used solely for the purposes described in this agreement and is not sold or disclosed to third parties except as described in Section 15.

d) Virginia (VCDPA), Colorado (CPA), Connecticut (CTDPA), Indiana, Kentucky, Rhode Island
You may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your data, and to opt out of certain processing activities. As described in Section 3, the automated profiling conducted by this platform is integral to the service and cannot be opted out of without terminating your membership.

e) Right to Delete
You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time through the Settings screen. See Section 14 for the 30-day retention and purge process.

f) Right to Data Export (Portability)
You may request an export of your personal data, including conversation transcripts and account information. Analytical overlays and proprietary algorithmic outputs are not included in data exports as they are platform-owned intellectual property.

17. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY (COPPA COMPLIANCE)

Sovereign Sanctuary does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Children aged 13-17 may only access the platform as dependents on a parent/guardian's family account, subject to parental consent and supervision. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 13 without parental consent, we will delete that information promptly.

PART III — THERAPEUTIC SETTING WAIVER

18. NATURE OF THE SERVICE

Sovereign Sanctuary operates in a space adjacent to — but distinct from — licensed mental health care. It is essential that you understand the following:

  • The platform is NOT a licensed mental health provider in any jurisdiction
  • Little Nate is NOT a therapist. Little Nate is an AI companion designed to support emotional exploration and self-awareness. Interactions with Little Nate do not constitute therapy, counseling, or medical treatment
  • Coaches are independent practitioners. Some coaches on the platform may hold professional licenses (LMFT, LCSW, LPC, etc.), but their use of the Sovereign Sanctuary platform does not automatically establish a formal therapeutic relationship governed by their licensing board's standards of care
  • No doctor-patient or therapist-client privilege applies to your interactions with Little Nate

19. INFORMED CONSENT FOR EXPERIMENTAL METHODOLOGY

The Nevedal Quantum Emotional Coherence framework is a research model and proprietary analytical methodology. It is NOT a clinically validated diagnostic tool approved by the FDA, APA, or any regulatory body.

You understand and acknowledge that:

  • Emotional coherence scores (C_emo) are algorithmic estimates, not clinical measurements
  • The Predictability Model of Behavior (PMB) is a pattern-recognition tool, not a psychological assessment instrument
  • CEE Window detection is based on statistical thresholds, not clinical criteria
  • Crisis detection algorithms may produce false positives or false negatives
  • Biometric analysis (voice and facial) provides indicators of emotional states but should not be relied upon as medical or psychiatric evidence
