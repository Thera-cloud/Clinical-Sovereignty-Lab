13a. Third-Party AI Service Disclosure

Sovereign Sanctuary uses third-party AI providers (including xAI (Grok) and Microsoft Azure OpenAI Service, and other configured inference providers) for the AI companion (Little Nate). The following data may be transmitted during AI use:

  • Text messages: Your typed or voice-transcribed messages are sent to the active AI provider(s) to generate Little Nate's conversational responses.
  • Conversation context: Recent conversation history (within the current session) is included for contextual relevance.
  • Identifiers in message content: Message content is sent as you type or speak it. If you include names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other identifiers in a message, those identifiers are included in what is transmitted. Message content is not scrubbed of personal identifiers before AI processing on the primary chat path.
  • Voice biometric features: Extracted voice characteristics (pitch mean, pitch variance, energy level, speech rate, and pause ratio) may be processed server-side for emotional analysis on instrumented voice/live paths. Raw audio is not sent to Azure OpenAI as a separate media upload for that analysis path.
  • Session metadata: Derived emotional state indicators (mood scores, heuristic metrics) may be included in AI prompts to personalize responses.

Data not sent as separate profile/credential fields to AI providers:

  • Passwords and authentication credentials
  • Payment card information (handled exclusively by Stripe)
  • Raw audio or video recordings as media files (voice features may be transcribed to text first)

Important: Profile fields such as name, email, and phone are not guaranteed to be stripped from AI prompts. Any personal information you include in conversation text is transmitted to the AI provider on the primary chat path.

Protections provided by Microsoft Azure OpenAI:

  • Your data is NOT used to train, retrain, or improve Azure OpenAI foundation models
  • Your prompts and completions are NOT available to other customers or to OpenAI
  • Data is processed within Microsoft Azure's SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified infrastructure
  • Enterprise-level data processing agreements (DPA) are in place

Your consent: Before your first AI chat interaction, the app presents an explicit consent dialog explaining what data is sent, who it is sent to, and your rights. You may decline, in which case AI chat functionality will be unavailable. You can revoke consent at any time via Settings > Privacy > AI Data Consent, which will disable AI chat features.
