How You Opt In

On the Sovereign Sanctuary signup form at https://app.sovereignsanctuary.net, you enter your own mobile number and may check an optional, unchecked-by-default box:

Consent is not required to create an account or use the platform. We collect consent only from the owner of the number. After you opt in, you receive a confirmation SMS.

Typical frequency is 1 to 4 messages per month and varies with account activity (for example, scheduled sessions).

Message and data rates may apply. Your carrier’s standard rates govern delivery.
